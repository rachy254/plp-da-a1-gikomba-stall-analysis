## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku runs a local fresh vegetable stall. To maximize her profits and reduce wastage, she needs a structured way to analyze her weekly sales, categorize high-performing days, and organize her inventory. This project provides a pure Python, non-pandas/non-numpy solution to compute her metrics, categorize performance, sort revenues, and generate structured operational reports.

## How to Run
1. Open Google Colab (or any standard Jupyter notebook environment).
2. Upload the notebook file `Wanjikus_Stall_Analysis.ipynb`.
3. Run all cells sequentially from top to bottom (`Runtime` -> `Run all`).
4. Observe the console outputs, which include the detailed, formatted Stall Performance Report.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Peak Sales Day**: Saturday is the most lucrative day, generating **4,000.00 KES** from Potatoes alone (80 units at 50 KES/unit).
2. **Lowest Sales Day**: Wednesday recorded the lowest revenue of **1,600.00 KES** selling Cabgages, signaling potential for targeted promotions.
3. **Weekly Revenue Benchmark**: The stall successfully generated a total weekly revenue of **18,350.00 KES**, establishing a baseline for future sales targets.
